Ingest

The latest 10-K, four 10-Qs and a year of 8-Ks are pulled from SEC EDGAR, parsed section-by-section and indexed.

02

Research

Specialist agents query the filings, fetch market data and scan news — in parallel, guided by your focus area.

03

Compute

Every financial number is calculated deterministically from XBRL data. The AI never does the math.

04

Verify

A critic checks every claim against its cited source. Anything unverifiable is revised or dropped — and disclosed.
